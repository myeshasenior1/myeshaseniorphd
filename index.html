Human Factors Engineering · Health Equity · Sociotechnical Design

Designing systems that make equitable care easier to access from a patient perspective.

I am a PhD researcher in Human Factors Engineering at the University of Toronto. My work examines how people, care systems, and technologies interact to shape patient work—and how those systems can be redesigned with communities rather than merely for them.

About me

I care about the space between what a system intends to do and patient's expectations to better understand the patient experience and work.

My research asks how health systems can be redesigned so that equity is not dependent on an individual patient’s ability to navigate complexity, advocate for themselves, or absorb additional work.

Outside of research, I enjoy photography, videography, and DJing with friends.
